## 4. Rain source: IMERG Final vs POWER (2024, pilot area)

2024 total: POWER **652 mm**, IMERG **410 mm** (37% less); daily correlation 0.37. The sources disagree strongly, so the app shows POWER as its source and states the dependency. Effect on one season (cowpea planted in dekad 12 of 2024): ETa/ETc = 1.00 with POWER rain, 0.47 with IMERG rain (success needs 0.80).

## Assumptions behind these numbers

- Available water per soil class: {1: 0.08, 2: 0.11, 3: 0.14} m³/m³ (class 2 = proposal premise; 1 and 3 are project assumptions).
- Initial moisture 60% of available water, effective rain equals rain, no irrigation.
- Success: ETa/ETc ≥ 0.80 and mid-season (flowering) deficit < 25 mm. Dekad 1 = Jan 1-10 (unconfirmed).
- Kc and root depth curves are assumptions recorded in `crop_parameter_sources`.
